\section{Statistical comparisons}
\label{app:stats}

\paragraph{Translation metrics.}
mWERSegmenter~\citep{matusov-etal-2005-evaluating} resegments the hypotheses
to the StreamLAAL references~\citep{papi2024streamatt} before we compute
corpus SacreBLEU~\citep{papineni-etal-2002-bleu,post-2018-call}.
Masked-term BLEU, or MT-BLEU, repeats scoring with every evaluated target
term masked in both hypothesis and reference.

\paragraph{Paired tests.}
Table~\ref{tab:stats} reports AutoTerm-SST minus each baseline. The exact
permutation test swaps outputs between systems within each talk and recomputes
the pooled metric for all 1,024 combinations. The smallest
attainable two-sided $p$-value is 0.002. Significance claims rest on this test.
With ten talks, the percentile bootstrap can disagree with it near the boundary.
For MT-BLEU against similarity routing, the interval is below $0$ while $p=0.13$.
Confidence intervals come from 4,000 talk-level bootstrap resamples
stratified by corpus, with five ACL and five ESO talks. We write them as
$a\pm h$ when symmetric and $a^{+u}_{-l}$ otherwise.
When any single talk is removed, AutoTerm-SST still leads Merged-1M by at least
2.57 TERM\_ACC, 2.04 BLEU, and 1.70 MT-BLEU points.

\section{Router configuration}
\label{app:config}

Beyond the shared settings in Table~\ref{tab:config}, the language-model router
adds the switch rate $\rho$ and the similarity router adds five settings. The
latter uses BGE-M3~\citep{chen-etal-2024-m3} to embed the last 128 tokens of $c_t$
and each prototype with its Chinese translation. It pins the confirmed slice
and switches after a candidate leads both the runner-up and the confirmed slice
by fixed margins for three consecutive updates outside a 30-second cooldown.
We tuned the thresholds on four short segments from the evaluation talks before
running the complete streams. Each router ran on its own server because
the updates use wall-clock time.

\section{Router diagnostics}
\label{app:router}

Open-loop replays give both deployed routers the same saved ACL-ESO
translations and use the context in Section~\ref{sec:adaptive-memory}.
As in live runs, scores refresh at most once every 5 seconds of audio.
Each refresh applies the similarity router's confirmation rule and pin,
or one language-model filter step. The similarity router reproduces 19 topic
switches from the live run when given the original emission times.

\paragraph{Short topic shifts.}
The posterior follows topics alternating every 15 to 300 seconds. For each
length in Table~\ref{tab:router-diag}, we interleave saved NLP and medicine
translation segments into one 40-segment stream. At each length, at least
half the segments start with the incoming slice in the language-model router's
working set. The slice ranks first after a median of 7.7 seconds. On segments
at least one minute long, similarity routing confirms the incoming slice
after a median of 27 to 46 seconds.

\paragraph{Close domains.}
Finer slices widen the routing gap. The same 4B model splits each of NLP
and medicine into eight subtopics. It proposes subtopics from 400 terms
outside the evaluation glossaries and assigns terms by letter probability.
The other eight topics stay in the catalog. Table~\ref{tab:router-diag}
tracks working-set coverage of the topic slices for 2,047 gold term
occurrences. Selecting the best four slices per talk in hindsight gives
97.3\% coverage of the gold term occurrences.

\paragraph{Other target languages.}
Routing coverage carries over to German and Japanese in replays of the same
talks with RASST translations and only the prompt language changed.

\paragraph{Generated prototypes and late updates.}
GPT-5.5 wrote one prototype per slice from its glossary with evaluation
terms removed. With per-chunk updates on ACL-ESO, the generated prototypes
lower the language-model router's top-four coverage to 97.9\%. RealSI uses
them because its NLP talk shares topics with the hand-written NLP prototype.
Table~\ref{tab:realsi-orders} gives both routers' coverage and top-1 accuracy
by stream order. Closed-loop runs in Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} scored
updates before the next chunk. The deployed worker in
Section~\ref{sec:adaptive-memory} can apply an update one chunk late when
audio arrives faster than real time. For the replays in Table~\ref{tab:router-diag},
the delay reduces coverage by up to 0.33 points.
