\section{Conclusion}

AutoTerm-SST routes from partial translations to offline topic slices for
terminology-aware streaming translation without glossary upload or domain
selection. It matches a known-domain reference, avoids losses from a
million-entry merged glossary, and tracks topic changes across ten domains.
Scaling the router from tens of slices to thousands, with an embedding
shortlist or a topic hierarchy ahead of the letter prompt, is a natural next step.

\section*{Ethics statement}
We evaluate on the published ACL 60/60, ESO test set, and RealSI corpora,
using RealSI under CC BY 4.0 without redistributing its audio.
