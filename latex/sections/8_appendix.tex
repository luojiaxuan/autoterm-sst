\section{Statistical comparisons}
\label{app:stats}

Paired talk-level tests show that the gains of AutoTerm-SST over Merged-1M
hold across individual talks. Table~\ref{tab:stats} reports every paired
comparison. The exact permutation test swaps the outputs of two systems within
each talk across all 1,024 combinations and recomputes the pooled metric,
giving a minimum attainable two-sided $p$-value of 0.002. Significance claims
rest on this test. With ten talks, the percentile bootstrap can disagree with
it near the boundary: for Merged-10k the interval excludes zero while
$p=0.084$. Confidence intervals
come from 4,000 stratified bootstrap resamples of five ACL and five ESO talks.
Removing any single talk preserves all three advantages of AutoTerm-SST over
Merged-1M, with gains of at least 3.28 TERM\_ACC points, 2.00 BLEU, and 1.99
MT-BLEU.

\section{Router diagnostics}
\label{app:router}

Open-loop replays of the routing signal isolate the benefit of pinning,
measure responsiveness to rapid topic shifts, and test LLM-generated
prototypes. Each diagnostic replays the router on the saved translations of
run 1 without decoding again, encoding the last four translated chunks as in
Section~\ref{sec:adaptive-memory} and ranking slices by raw similarity without
the confirmation rule or pinning. Under raw similarity alone, the correct slice
ranks among the top four in 85.0\% of the 8,776 chunks. Keeping the live run's
confirmed slice in the working set and filling the remaining three slots by raw
rank raises coverage to 97.0\%, matching the 96.9\% measured live.

\paragraph{Short topic shifts.}
The raw-similarity ranking follows topic switches quickly even when domains
alternate every 15 to 300 seconds. For each length in Table~\ref{tab:router-diag},
we cut the saved translations of the NLP and medicine talks into segments of
that length and interleave them into one 40-segment stream. At every length, the
incoming topic slice enters the top four after a median of one chunk, or 1.92
seconds, and ranks first after a median of 5.76 seconds. It reaches the top
four in all 40 segments at every length, and Table~\ref{tab:router-diag} shows
that top-four coverage stays above 81\% even when topics switch every 15
seconds.

\paragraph{Generated prototypes.}
LLM-generated prototypes route about as well as hand-written ones. For each
slice, we remove entries originating from the evaluation glossaries and give
the remaining term pairs to \texttt{gpt-5.5-2026-04-23}, which writes a topic
description, seven to ten core topics, and their Chinese translation in the
format of the hand-written prototypes. Across the ten independently generated sets in Table~\ref{tab:router-diag},
the lowest and highest sets bracket the hand-written prototypes on both Top-4
and Top-1.
