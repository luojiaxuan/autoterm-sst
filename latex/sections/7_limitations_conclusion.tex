\section{Conclusion}

AutoTerm-SST routes its partial translations to offline topic slices, removing
glossary upload and domain selection from terminology-aware streaming
translation. It matches a known-domain reference, avoids losses from a
million-entry merged glossary, and follows topic changes across ten domains.
Routing to finer subtopics is a natural next step.
