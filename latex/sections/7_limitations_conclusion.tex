\section{Conclusion}

AutoTerm-SST removes glossary upload and domain selection from
terminology-aware streaming translation: a small language model routes the
system's own partial translations to offline topic slices under a fixed
budget. It matches a known-domain reference, avoids the losses of a
million-entry merged glossary, follows topic changes across ten domains, and
shows its terminology evidence live. Extending the router to finer-grained
subtopics is a natural next step.
