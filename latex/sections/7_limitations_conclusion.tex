\section{Conclusion}

AutoTerm-SST removes glossary upload and domain selection from
terminology-aware streaming translation by routing its own partial
translations to offline topic slices under a fixed budget. It is on par with a
known-domain reference in two independent runs, avoids the losses of a
million-entry merged glossary, and shows its terminology evidence live. Extending the router to more target languages and finer-grained
subtopics is a natural next step.
