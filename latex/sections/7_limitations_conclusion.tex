\section{Conclusion}

AutoTerm-SST removes glossary upload and domain selection from
terminology-aware streaming translation by routing its own partial
translations to offline topic slices. It matches a known-domain reference,
avoids the losses of a million-entry merged glossary, and follows topic
changes across ten domains. Extending the router to finer-grained subtopics is
a natural next step.
