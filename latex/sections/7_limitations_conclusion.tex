\section{Conclusion}

AutoTerm-SST removes glossary upload and domain selection from
terminology-aware streaming translation. It reads the topic from its own
partial translation, searches a few topic slices under a fixed budget, and
shows the evidence behind every partial output. On a mixed ACL and oncology
stream it matches a known-domain reference in two independent runs and avoids
the losses of a one-million-entry merged glossary. Extending the router to more
target languages and to finer-grained subtopics is a natural next step.
