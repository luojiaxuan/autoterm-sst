\section{Statistical comparisons}
\label{app:stats}

Table~\ref{tab:stats} lists every paired comparison. The permutation test
swaps the outputs of the two systems within each talk over all 1,024
combinations and recomputes the pooled metric, so the smallest attainable
two-sided $p$ is 0.002. The intervals come from 4,000 bootstrap resamples of
five ACL and five ESO talks. Removing any single talk keeps all three
advantages of AutoTerm-SST over Merged-1M positive, at least 3.28 TERM\_ACC
points, 2.00 BLEU, and 1.99 MT-BLEU.

\section{Router diagnostics}
\label{app:router}

These diagnostics replay the routing signal on the saved translations of run 1
without decoding again. Each window encodes the last four translated chunks as
in Section~\ref{sec:adaptive-memory} and ranks the slices by raw similarity,
without the confirmation rule and pinning of the live router. In this raw
ranking the correct slice is among the four most similar slices in 85.0\% of
the 8,776 windows. Keeping the confirmed slice of the live run in the working
set and filling the other three slots by raw rank raises this to 97.0\%, which
matches the 96.9\% measured in the live run.

\paragraph{Short topic shifts.}
We cut the translations of the five ACL and five medicine talks into segments
of 15 to 300 seconds and interleave NLP and medicine segments into one stream
of 40 segments per length. At every length, the slice of the new topic enters
the four most similar slices a median of one chunk, 1.92 seconds, after the
switch and ranks first after a median of 5.76 seconds. It reaches the top four
in all 40 segments at every length. Table~\ref{tab:router-diag} shows that
coverage stays above 81\% even when the topic changes every 15 seconds.

\paragraph{Generated prototypes.}
Prototypes can be produced without hand-writing. For each slice we give all
of its term pairs, after removing entries that come from the evaluation
glossaries, to \texttt{gpt-5.5-2026-04-23} and ask for a description, a list of
seven to ten core topics, and its Chinese translation, in the same format as
the hand-written prototypes. We generate ten independent prototype sets.
Table~\ref{tab:router-diag} shows that their routing accuracy brackets that of
the hand-written prototypes.
